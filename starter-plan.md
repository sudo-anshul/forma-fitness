# Forma beginner training guide

This is a general starting framework for a healthy adult, not a personal medical or nutrition prescription. Use a comfortable range of motion and ask a trainer to demonstrate unfamiliar equipment. Adjust training and food targets to your circumstances.

## A manageable week

Alternate full-body strength workouts A and B on Monday, Wednesday and Friday. The next week begins with the other workout. Use Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday for easy walking, cycling or recovery. Sunday can be a rest day. Most strength sessions take about 45–60 minutes including warm-up and rests.

Warm up with approximately five minutes of easy activity and light practice sets. Choose loads that leave two or three controlled repetitions in reserve. Rest around two minutes between main-exercise sets. Stop movements that cause joint pain.

| Workout A | Working sets × repetitions |
|---|---|
| Dumbbell goblet squat | 2 × 8–12 |
| Machine chest press | 2 × 8–12 |
| One-arm supported dumbbell row | 2 × 8–12 each side |
| Dumbbell Romanian deadlift, with instruction | 2 × 8–12 |
| Light dumbbell lateral raise | 1–2 × 12–15 |
| Dead bug | 2 × 6–10 each side |

| Workout B | Working sets × repetitions |
|---|---|
| Dumbbell goblet squat | 2 × 8–12 |
| Lat pulldown | 2 × 8–12 |
| Machine chest press | 2 × 8–12 |
| Dumbbell Romanian deadlift, with instruction | 2 × 8–12 |
| Dumbbell biceps curl | 1–2 × 10–15 |
| Standing calf raise, holding a stable support | 2 × 12–20 |

If Romanian-deadlift instruction is unavailable, use a comfortable bodyweight glute bridge temporarily; it is not an identical hamstring exercise. Keep calf raises controlled, without bouncing. The machine's instructions and your trainer should determine chest-press seat and start-position settings.

## Progress and recovery

Log your repetitions and weights. Once all sets reach the top of the range with controlled technique and two or three repetitions still available on two consecutive occasions, try the smallest manageable load increase. Avoid adding load, sets and training days all at once.

Most adults need seven to nine hours of sleep. Reduce strenuous exercise when severely sleep-deprived or unwell. Persistent fatigue, pain or disrupted sleep deserves individual assessment.

## Food and measurements

The app's starting calorie, protein and profile values are editable examples. They are not calculated maintenance needs or measurements of the person using the app. Set your own profile and goals before relying on comparisons.

Eat regular meals with protein foods, vegetables or fruit, carbohydrate foods and dietary fat. Adjust portions using several weeks of weight, waist, energy and training trends. There is no exercise that selectively removes fat from the sides of the waist.

Food values are estimates. Use package labels when available, and distinguish dry from cooked weights. Include cooking oil when logging recipes. Low-fat milk still contains lactose; use foods you tolerate.
